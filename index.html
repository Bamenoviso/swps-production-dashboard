User Manual — Smart Workforce Planning System

Quick reference for navigating the dashboard.

1. Signing in

Choose your Role and Department on the sign-in screen, then enter any Employee ID and password (this is a proposal prototype demo, so any value works), or use "Continue as Guest" for a read-only-style preview.

2. Switching context

Everything you see — Registry, Scheduling, Capacity, Overtime, Deployment, and Pull-Out data — belongs to the Department you signed in under. Sign out and sign back in under a different department to see its own data. Wharf and Silo Department is pre-loaded with sample data from the FEP study; Flour Mill and Feed Mill start empty until their supervisors begin logging data.

3. Executive Dashboard

At-a-glance KPIs and a live Pull-Out summary. Use "Generate Report" to preview a formatted Weekly Progress Report built from the current department's data — download it as PDF, Word, or Excel, or use Save / Save Draft to store it in History.

4. Personnel Registry

Your worker directory. Use "+ Add Worker" to register a new employee under the current department.

5. Workforce Scheduling

Shows assigned vs. required headcount per shift. Use "Assign Worker" to add one assignment to a position/shift; shortages are flagged in red.

6. Capacity Planning

Actual vs. required headcount per position. Use "+ New Plan" to add a position to the capacity — and Pull-Out — scope.

7. Overtime Monitoring

Log overtime with "+ Log Overtime"; download the current log with "Export Report" (CSV).

8. Personnel Deployment

Live floor view. "Record Deployment" adds a manual entry; ongoing Pull-Outs appear here automatically.

9. Pull-Out Management

Requirements sets Required Headcount and Trigger Threshold per position → Minimum Before Trigger is computed automatically. Daily Tracker flags shortages in red — double-click a red cell to log a pull-out. The Pull-Out Log records every event, and Summary & Escalation flags any position with 3+ pull-outs in a month for a manning review.

10. Report Center

Build a custom report: set a title, department label, reporting period/date, prepared-by, and remarks, then click "Build Preview" to see it rendered with this department's live data. Once built, use Save / Save Draft to store it in History, or export as PDF, Word, or Excel.

11. History

Lists every report you've saved (via "Save" or "Save Draft" from Generate Report or the Report Center), with date, department, Draft/Saved status, and who prepared it. Use the search box to find one quickly, "View" to reopen and export it, or "Del" to remove it.

12. Your profile

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to rename yourself, toggle preferences, or sign out.
